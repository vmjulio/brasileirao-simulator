The four inherited constants

None was ever fitted. All four sit at or near their optimum. The model is hurt by discarding history and never by keeping more.

default, at zerovariant· pill = seasons the variant beat the default, of 10

Brier here is the original 1950 form summed over three outcomes, range 0–2; differences are variant minus default, so positive is worse. The eight-of-ten rule is the bar: a variant counts only if it beats the default in at least 8 of the 10 seasons. Nothing reaches it.

Same lambdas, sampled instead of fixed

Parameter uncertainty (the uncertain adapter)

Drawing each simulated season's lambdas from a Gamma centred on the point estimate loses to fixed lambda. No per-season export was kept for it, so it has no chart here.

It adds variance around the mean without moving the mean. If the remaining error is systematic bias, symmetric noise cannot correct it; it only blunts sharpness, which a proper scoring rule punishes. It was aimed at the wrong error.

Definitions

How to read these charts

RPS
Ranked Probability Score. Home, draw and away are ordered, so a near miss (draw forecast, home win) costs less than a far one (away forecast, home win). The standard for 1X2 forecasts. Lower is better.
Brier (0–2)
Sum of squared errors over the three outcomes. Treats the outcomes as unrelated labels. Published figures near 0.2–0.3 are usually halved or divided by three; check the divisor before comparing.
Horizon 0
Each match is scored with the last forecast made strictly before kick-off, a median of one day out.
Paired difference
Both models score the same match; the difference is averaged and bootstrapped over matches. An interval that excludes zero is a real gap at 95%.
Eight of ten
A variant must beat the default in at least 8 of 10 completed seasons. It separated the real lookback result (0 of 10) from a fake one (6 of 10, noise). Six-season results cannot meet it and are labelled provisional.
Base rate
“Home wins 47% of the time in this league” and nothing about which clubs are playing.
