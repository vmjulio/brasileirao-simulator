## Rows dropped, per rule, and why

Applied in order: league membership (silent — a league id absent from `COMPETITIONS`
is never read even if its shard exists, proven with a temp-root test), status, then
round.

| league | input rows | dropped: status | dropped: round | admitted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 71 | 9,166 | 1 (`CANC`) | 0 (no round filter) | 9,165 |
| 72 | 4,940 | 10 (`NS`) | 0 (no round filter) | 4,930 |
| 73 | 1,215 | 9 (`PST`+`CANC`) | 912 (`1st`–`4th Round`) | 294 |
| 13 | 1,085 | 1 (`AWD`) | 210 (`1st`–`3rd Round`) | 874 |
| 11 | 950 | 80 (`NS`+`TBD`) | 264 (`1st`–`2nd Round`) | 606 |

Status filter admits `FT`/`AET`/`PEN` only, dropping `CANC`, `PST`, `NS`, `TBD`, `AWD`
everywhere. Round filter (`73`→`ROUND_OF_16`, `13`/`11`→`GROUP_STAGE`) drops every row
whose `league_round` ranks below the competition's cutoff on the stage scale
`domain/competitions.py` defines (`_stage_rank`): both cups' numbered preliminary
rounds (`"1st Round"` … `"4th Round"`) rank below every group/knockout label, including
both Copa do Brasil spellings of the round of 16 (`"Round of 16"`, `"8th Finals"`, 80
rows admitted under each label) and both group-stage label styles Libertadores has used
(`"Group A - N"`…`"Group H - N"` and `"Group Stage - N"`).

None of the 165 raw PEN/AET rows were dropped for a missing 90-minute score — that
count (73:79, 13:36, 11:50) is unaffected by round inclusion in the sense that scoring
is resolved before round-admission changes anything about *which* rows exist; round
inclusion is simply why only 65 of those 165 end up in the default `COMPETITIONS`
store (`tests/test_match_store.py::test_all_165_pen_and_aet_rows_are_scored_from_fulltime_not_goals`
checks all 165 against a rules dict with every round filter lifted, isolating the
scoring rule from the round-inclusion rule).

**One real data-quality wrinkle, handled and documented in `match_store.py`:**
3,126 admitted rows — every one a plain `FT` Série A match, mostly 2008 and earlier —
have no `score_fulltime_*` at all in the export. Since `FT` means no extra time was
played, `goals_*` and the missing fulltime score are the same number by definition for
that status only, so `_resolve_fulltime_score` fills the gap from `goals_*` there and
nowhere else; an `AET`/`PEN` row missing its fulltime score would raise instead of
guessing (never triggered — the ticket's own audit and this run both confirm all 165
PEN/AET rows carry a real fulltime score).

## `is_neutral`

Heuristic, documented in `match_store.py`'s module docstring: each club's usual home
venue is the modal `fixture_venue_id` among its own admitted home matches (ties broken
by the smallest venue id, deterministically). A match is flagged neutral when its venue
matches **neither** side's usual ground. Two limitations, both biased toward
under-flagging (the safer direction for a signal that only nudges home advantage):

1. `fixture_venue_id` is missing on ~60% of admitted rows (older Série A seasons,
   in-progress ones) — a missing venue can never be shown to differ from a usual
   ground, so it defaults to **not** neutral.
2. A club with no admitted home match at a known venue (a foreign
   Libertadores/Sudamericana side that only ever travels to Brazil in this data) has no
   usual ground to compare against, so any known venue counts as "not this club's" —
   which can tip a match to neutral on weak grounds.

**Flagged 624 of 15,869 admitted matches (3.9%) as neutral**, 9,993 more had no venue
and defaulted to not-neutral, and 180 clubs got a known usual venue. Spot-checked
against a real case: fixture 801186, the 2021 Libertadores Final (Palmeiras 1-1
Flamengo at 90 minutes, Estadio Centenario, Montevideo), is flagged `is_neutral=True` —
asserted directly in the test.
